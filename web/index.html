One-Blind-Mouse

Point at things on each other's screens while you talk.

One-Blind-Mouse is a small, portable Windows tray app. Share a short code with the people on your call, and everyone's mouse pointer appears on everyone else's screen, each with its own name and colour.

It is a way to say "this one, here". It does not control anyone's computer and does not share your screen.

Download for Windows

How it works

  1. Download and run One-Blind-Mouse. There is nothing to install.
  2. Click the tray icon and share your code, for example K7QM-4PZX.
  3. The others paste the code into their copy of the app, or open your join link.
